# Authors

## Authors

People who have made a substantial contribution to the architecture, such as writing or
rewriting a section, or a change that shaped the model. Authors appear on the paper and in
the citation, in order of contribution.

- Luke Hinds, nolabs, Inc

## Contributors

People whose review, errata or pull requests improved a draft. Listed alphabetically, with
the draft their contribution first appeared in.

## How to be listed

Contributors are added when their change or review is merged into a draft. Authorship is
agreed with the existing authors before a release, usually after a substantial
contribution has been discussed in an issue or pull request. Tell us in your issue or pull
request if you would rather not be listed, or want a different name or affiliation used.

The author list is maintained in one place: the `**Authors:**` line at the top of
[`paper/paper.md`](paper/paper.md), in the form `Name (Affiliation), Name (Affiliation)`.
The site byline, the PDF title page and the BibTeX are generated from it. When it changes,
also update `CITATION.cff` (`authors` and `preferred-citation.authors`) and the list above.
